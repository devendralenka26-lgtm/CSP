Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common queries about AgriAI smart tools.

Does the platform require internet?

No, AgriAI utilizes PWA caching. You can run voice assistant keywords, check fertilizer logs, and view previous disease scans fully offline.

How does the Leaf Scanner work?

Our backend applies OpenCV to segment leaf borders, isolate lesion spots (yellowing/spots), and passes these features to a Scikit-learn Classifier.

What languages are supported?

It supports 11 languages including Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, English, Bengali, Marathi, Punjabi, Gujarati, and Odia.

Can I connect soil sensors?

Yes. The dashboard comes with a Future-Ready Drone & IoT console ready to handle real telemetry feeds from hardware units.
